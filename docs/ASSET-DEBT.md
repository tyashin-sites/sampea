# Asset debt — what the customer still needs to provide

| Item | Needed for |
|---|---|
| Sampéa wordmark or logo (SVG) | Header, footer, favicon and social share image |
| Brand tagline (to confirm, none provided) | Brand tagline field and social share metadata |
| Legal review of privacy policy and terms for India, Canada and the US | /privacy and /terms before go-live |
| Postal address, phone, WhatsApp number and opening hours | Contact page details, footer and search-engine listings. No address was provided, so the address line stays off the contact page and footer rather than showing a placeholder. |
| **How long the scent lasts, in words you are happy to publish** | The brief says "40% oil concentration with fragrance staying for week(s)". One week or several reads very differently, and a longevity claim needs to be one you can stand behind, so nothing about duration is on the site yet. Confirm the exact wording and it goes on the home page next to the 40% figure. |
| **The rules for a personalised bottle** | The home page now says a name can go on a bottle and that nobody else can order it. Before that can be built: how many characters a name may be, which characters are allowed, what happens when two people want the same name, whether a name is held forever or released, what it costs, and whether it is offered in all three markets. |

## Images

The four bottle images on the site are renders of the `Perfume.glb` model the
customer supplied, served from the Tyashin media library:

| Render | Where it is used |
|---|---|
| Three-quarter front (1800x1350) | Home, hero |
| Close detail of the cap (1800x1800) | Home, "The only choice left is the scent" |
| Straight front (1440x1800) | Home, "Your name on the bottle" |
| Side profile (1800x1013) | About, "Two fixed numbers, then the scents" |

Two things to note:

- These are renders, not photographs of a produced bottle. If the finished
  bottle differs from the model, the pictures need replacing before launch.
- The `.glb` model itself is not on the site. Interactive 3D on a Tyashin site
  comes from the Thridify plugin, which is not installed on this project.
